The Problem

Managing rental properties is complex and time-consuming. Landlords struggle with tracking payments, communicating with tenants, and organizing property records.

  • Manual tracking leads to errors and disputes
  • Payment verification is difficult and slow
  • Tenant communication is fragmented
  • No central system for rent history
✨

Our Solution

Rentify provides a comprehensive platform that connects landlords and tenants with powerful tools for rent management, payments, and communication.

  • Real-time rent status tracking
  • Integrated payment system with instant receipts
  • Role-based flows for landlords and tenants
  • Complete payment history in the cloud
